Project highlights

Clarified founder-to-investor flows, simplified pitching steps, and created a scalable UI kit for profiles, funding rounds, and discovery.

UX Research Wireframes UI Kit Prototyping

Problem

Entrepreneurs struggle to reach the right investors, while investors lack a clean, comparable way to evaluate opportunities quickly. The market is fragmented, and founders often lose time across scattered channels.

Case study

StepUp organizes discovery, profiles, and pitching into one focused experience. Founders showcase traction and goals, investors filter by sector and stage, and both sides connect with fewer steps and clearer signals.
